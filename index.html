Pricing questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. When you upgrade, the new features are available straight away, and the price difference is adjusted on your next bill.

What does the 30-day money-back guarantee cover?

If you are not happy with your plan within the first 30 days, you can ask for a full refund. No questions asked.

Is there a free trial?

The Pro plan includes a 14-day free trial, so you can try daily backups and extra storage before you pay.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay once for the full year and save 20% compared with paying monthly. The yearly total is shown under each price.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking.

Do you offer help with moving my website?

Yes. Pro and Business plans include free website migration, and our team will move your files for you.
